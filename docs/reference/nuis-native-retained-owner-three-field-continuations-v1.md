## Existing Owner Authority

The classifier receives the existing opaque `Target`; it never creates one.
Two known writes including the owner still take the unchanged pair route.
Two known writes excluding the owner can receive the three-field certificate
only when both children are prefix-defined, mutable, exact numeric values,
absent from the entering scope and not sealed. Owner-plus-two-child writes
retain the preceding admission. Three private children without the owner,
other entering writes, missing/sealed/qualified owners and children reject.

Root discovery still requires actual original owner-write evidence. A root
body that only updates two children is rejected, even when an owner-shaped
value exists in its scope. An already delegated target may retain its current
value through a child-only stage; retention is not another source permission.

The original helper returns bool; exactly one numeric target reaches its
enclosing continuation. Both private children stay in their certified lexical
region. Initial arms, nested child selections and the shared suffix are
visited once under the original forest, expression, statement, depth and
capture budgets. No continuation is copied into both arms.
